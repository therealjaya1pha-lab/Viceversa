Reading is a complex cognitive process involving visual recognition, auditory processing, and motor output. For children diagnosed with dyslexia or phonological delays, standard whole-language reading methods can leave significant gaps in decoding and spelling.

At **Vice Versa Speech and Language Services**, we incorporate the **Orton-Gillingham (OG)** methodology into our reading and literacy programs to build confident, fluent readers.

## Core Principles of the Orton-Gillingham Method

Orton-Gillingham is an explicit, systematic, and multi-sensory approach designed specifically for students who struggle with reading:

### 1. Multi-Sensory Engagement (VAK)
Lessons simultaneously engage **Visual** (seeing letters), **Auditory** (hearing sounds), and **Kinesthetic-Tactile** (tracing letters in sand, tapping out phonemes) pathways to reinforce memory and neural mapping.

### 2. Sequential & Cumulative Progression
Reading instruction begins with basic phonemes (individual letter sounds) and systematically builds to complex syllable types, prefixes, suffixes, and morphemes.

### 3. Diagnostic & Individualized Focus
Therapists closely monitor the child's mastery of each phonemic rule before advancing, ensuring zero guesswork and solid foundational confidence.

## Signs Your Child May Benefit from Orton-Gillingham

- Difficulty sounding out simple 3-letter words (CVC words like *cat*, *dog*, *pin*).
- Reversing letters (*b* vs *d*, *p* vs *q*) beyond age 7.
- Frustration during daily homework or school reading time.
- Struggles with spelling rules and phonemic awareness.
