Every child develops at their own unique rhythm, but understanding standard communication milestones allows parents to identify potential speech and language delays early when intervention is most effective.

At **Vice Versa Speech and Language Services** in Irving, TX, we frequently consult with parents who wonder whether their toddler is simply a "late talker" or if they require specialized clinical intervention.

## Key Communication Milestones by Age

### 12 to 18 Months
- **Gestures:** Points to objects of interest, waves bye-bye, and shakes head "no".
- **Vocalizations:** Uses at least 3-6 single words consistently (e.g., "mama", "dada", "ball", "more").
- **Imitation:** Attempts to imitate simple speech sounds and environmental noises.
- **Comprehension:** Responds to their name and understands simple 1-step requests like *"Come here"*.

### 18 to 24 Months
- **Vocabulary:** Uses 20 to 50+ meaningful words.
- **Word Combinations:** Begins pairing words together (e.g., *"more milk"*, *"go car"*).
- **Speech Clarity:** Family members understand at least 50% of what the child says.

### 24 to 36 Months
- **Sentence Structure:** Speaks in 3-4 word phrases regularly.
- **Questions:** Asks simple questions (*"Where daddy go?"*, *"What's that?"*).
- **Intelligibility:** Speech is understood 75% or more by familiar caregivers and educators.

---

## Red Flags That warrant an SLP Evaluation

If your child exhibits any of the following signs, scheduling a pediatric speech-language evaluation is highly recommended:

1. **Not responding to their name** or loud sounds by 12 months.
2. **Lack of babbling** or pointing gestures by 14 months.
3. **Fewer than 10 words** by 18 months.
4. **Loss of previously acquired words** or social engagement skills.
5. **Difficulty chewing or swallowing** soft foods without gagging.

## How Speech Therapy Helps

Our clinicians at Vice Versa utilize family-centered, play-based techniques to make therapy enjoyable and functional:

* **Interactive Modeling:** Teaching caregivers how to model target vocabulary naturally during daily play.
* **AAC Integration:** Introducing gesture systems or communication devices for children struggling with verbal output.
* **Parent Coaching:** Giving families practical tools to implement at home and in community settings.
